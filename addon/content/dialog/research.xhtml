Turn this into a structured review

A structured review follows a methodology (PRISMA, scoping review, Kitchenham SLR, …). You describe your goal in your own words or fill in the protocol yourself. Search results then flow through a funnel: a fast System 1 model estimates for every paper how likely it is to be relevant, an AI reasons about the uncertain ones, and you decide. Only papers that pass title/abstract screening are added to your Zotero collection.
